Object to 3D

Take a few photos of an object from different sides, or pick a short video. Depth Anything 3 (small) runs on this device and builds a point cloud you can rotate.

Pick 4 to 8 photos from different angles, or one short video that walks around the object. One photo also works, but it only gives the visible side.

Runs fully on this device. The model file (model/da3-small-fp16.onnx) is downloaded once (about 56 MB) and cached by the browser. Each photo is letterboxed to 504×504 so its proportions are kept. The cloud uses the model's own camera poses to place each view in one space. Depth Anything 3 weights (DA3-SMALL) are Apache-2.0.
