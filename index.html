Object to 3D

Take a few photos of an object from different sides, or pick a short video. Depth Anything 3 (small) runs on this device and builds a point cloud you can rotate.

Pick 4 to 8 photos from different angles, or one short video that walks around the object. One photo also works, but it only gives the visible side.
Use this after a crash or a bad result, and paste the text to the developer.
Show debug info

Runs fully on this device. The model file (model/da3-small-dyn-fp16.onnx) is downloaded once (about 55 MB) and cached by the browser. Each photo keeps its own aspect ratio (longest side 252–504 px, chosen to fit memory). The cloud uses the model's own camera poses to place each view in one space. Depth Anything 3 weights (DA3-SMALL) are Apache-2.0.
