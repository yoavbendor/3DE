Object to 3D

Take a few photos of an object from different sides, or pick a short video. Depth Anything 3 (small) runs on this device and builds a point cloud you can rotate.

Pick 3 to 8 photos from different angles, or one short video that walks around the object.

Runs fully on this device. The model file (model/da3-small-fp16.onnx) is loaded once and cached by the browser. Frames are squashed to 504×504 before inference, and the cloud uses the model's own camera poses to place each view in one space. Depth Anything 3 is Apache-2.0 (DA3-SMALL weights).
